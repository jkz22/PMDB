2 · The finding

Composition is identical. Arrangement is not.

All 51 scalar KPIs are batch-blind (best: KW p = 0.065). The signal is where the silicon sits: Batch 3 uniform · Batch 2 top-heavy, depleted mid-depth · Batch 1 bottom-heavy and variable.

Drying migration vs. sedimentation — physics you can act on, not a pixel artifact.{{fp_features}} curve-shape features
